Questions?

We've got answers.

Is Oinkk free?

Yes. Oinkk is free to download and use. Some extra games and pet outfits will be part of an optional premium plan.

Is it a dating app?

No. Oinkk is for people who are already together: couples, married or long distance. It's your private space as a pair.

Can anyone else see our stuff?

Never. Your memories, notes and chats are only visible to you and your partner.

We're in a long-distance relationship. Will it work for us?

It's made for you! Thumb kisses, time zones, distance tracker and games you can play from anywhere.

Which phones does it work on?

Android and iPhone. Both partners can use different phones, no problem.

When does it launch?

15 December 2026, just in time for Christmas. Join the list to get it first.
